Stamp Duty & LMI: The Upfront Costs Explained

schedule5 min read

Beyond your deposit, two costs catch many buyers by surprise: stamp duty and Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). Knowing them upfront helps you budget accurately.

What is stamp duty?

Stamp duty (transfer duty) is a state government tax on property purchases. In NSW the amount depends on the property price.

  • First home buyers may get an exemption or concession below certain price thresholds.
  • It's usually payable around settlement.

What is LMI?

Lenders Mortgage Insurance protects the lender (not you) when your deposit is below 20%. It can cost thousands and is often added to the loan.

How to reduce these costs

  • Save a 20% deposit to avoid LMI entirely.
  • Check first home buyer stamp duty concessions.
  • Some schemes (e.g. First Home Guarantee) let you skip LMI with a smaller deposit.

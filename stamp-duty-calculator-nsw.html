NSW stamp duty questions

When do I have to pay it?

Within three months of the date of exchange of contracts, or interest starts accruing. The date of exchange is the date shown on the contract. In practice most buyers pay at or just before settlement.

Can I add stamp duty to my home loan?

Generally no. Lenders treat it as an upfront cost you fund yourself, on top of your deposit. This is the single most common budgeting mistake we see — buyers save a 20% deposit, then discover the duty was never part of it.

What if I have owned property overseas but never in Australia?

The First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme test looks at residential property ownership in Australia. Owning property overseas does not by itself disqualify you — but the rules around prior interests, spouses and trusts are detailed, and the surcharge purchaser duty rules are separate. Worth confirming before you exchange.

Do foreign buyers pay more?

Yes. Surcharge purchaser duty of 9% of the dutiable value applies to foreign persons acquiring residential property in NSW, on top of standard transfer duty. The 9% rate applies to contracts entered into on or after 1 January 2025. Whether you count as a foreign person depends on visa and residency status, not nationality alone.

Why does the concession drop off so sharply above $800,000?

Because the relief phases out linearly between $800,000 and $1,000,000. At $800,000 you save the full duty; at $1,000,000 you save nothing. In the middle, every extra dollar of purchase price costs you roughly 15 cents in lost concession on top of the duty itself — which is why the band just above $800,000 deserves careful thought.

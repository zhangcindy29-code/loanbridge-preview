First Home Buyer Grants & Schemes in NSW (2026)

schedule7 min read · Updated August 2026

Buying your first home in NSW? There are three big schemes that can save you tens of thousands — and you can often use more than one together. Here's what each one gives you in 2026, and who qualifies.

1. The $10,000 First Home Owner Grant (FHOG)

The NSW First Home Owner Grant is a $10,000 cash payment — but only for brand-new homes: newly built, off-the-plan, or substantially renovated properties. It does not apply to established (existing) homes. Value caps apply to the property.

2. Stamp duty exemption (First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme)

Under the First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme (FHBAS), eligible first home buyers pay:

  • No stamp duty on a new or existing home up to $800,000.
  • A reduced (concessional) rate between $800,000 and $1,000,000.
  • Vacant land: exempt to $350,000, concessional to $450,000.

On an $800,000 home, that's roughly $30,000 you don't have to pay. Want your exact figure?

3. Buy with a 5% deposit — the federal First Home Guarantee

The federal First Home Guarantee (now often called the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme) lets eligible first home buyers purchase with as little as a 5% deposit and pay no Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) — the government guarantees the rest.

Big changes from 1 October 2025:

  • Income caps removed — no more $125k single / $200k couple limits.
  • Unlimited places — no annual cap on how many buyers can use it.
  • NSW property price cap raised to $1,500,000 (Sydney and regional centres).

For many first home buyers, this is the difference between buying now with 5% and waiting years to save a 20% deposit.

Can you use more than one at once?

Often, yes. A first home buyer purchasing a new home could receive the $10,000 grant, pay little or no stamp duty, and buy with a 5% deposit under the federal guarantee — all at once. Each scheme has separate eligibility, so the real question is which combination applies to your situation.

Who is generally eligible?

  • You're an individual (not a company or trust), at least 18, and an Australian citizen or permanent resident (rules vary by scheme).
  • You (and your partner) have never owned property in Australia before.
  • You'll move in and live in the home (usually within 12 months, for at least 12 months).
  • The property is within the relevant value / price caps.

Eligibility details differ between the state grants and the federal guarantee. A broker checks all of them for you in one go.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the First Home Owner Grant in NSW?

$10,000 for eligible buyers of a brand-new home (newly built, off-the-plan or substantially renovated). It does not apply to established homes. Value caps apply.

Do first home buyers pay stamp duty in NSW?

No duty on a home up to $800,000, and a reduced rate between $800,000 and $1,000,000. Vacant land is exempt to $350,000. Try our stamp duty calculator for your exact figure.

Can I buy with a 5% deposit?

Yes — under the federal First Home Guarantee, eligible first home buyers can buy with a 5% deposit and no LMI. Since 1 October 2025 there are no income caps or place limits, and the NSW price cap is $1,500,000.

Four ways to reduce or avoid LMI

savings

Reach a 20% deposit

The cleanest path. The calculator above shows the exact gap between your deposit and 20%. Sometimes a slightly cheaper property, or a few more months of saving, removes the premium entirely.

verified_user

Government guarantee schemes

Schemes such as the First Home Guarantee let eligible buyers purchase with a small deposit while the government guarantees the shortfall — so no LMI is charged. Places, income tests and property price caps apply and change over time.

badge

Professional waivers

Some lenders waive LMI entirely for certain occupations — commonly medical, legal, accounting and some other professions — at up to 90% or even 95% LVR. Which occupations qualify differs by lender, and the list is not advertised.

family_restroom

Family guarantee

A parent or close family member uses equity in their property as additional security, bringing your effective LVR under 80%. It removes LMI but puts the guarantor's property at risk, so it needs careful structuring.

Which of these you actually qualify for depends on the lender. Waiver lists, scheme eligibility and LMI rate tables all differ between lenders and are not published. This is the part a broker earns their keep on — comparing what each lender will actually do for your profile.

LMI questions people actually ask

Does LMI protect me as the borrower?

No. It protects the lender if you default and the property sells for less than what you owe. You pay the premium, but the cover belongs to the lender — and if the insurer pays out, it can still pursue you for the shortfall.

At what LVR does LMI kick in?

Most lenders require it above 80% LVR — that is, when your deposit is under 20% of the property value. The premium rises sharply as LVR climbs, which is why the jump from 90% to 95% costs so much more than the jump from 80% to 85%.

Can I add LMI to my loan instead of paying upfront?

Usually yes — this is called capitalising the premium. You avoid the upfront cash hit, but you pay interest on it for the life of the loan. The calculator above shows both the premium and what it adds to your monthly repayment if capitalised.

If I refinance later, do I pay LMI again?

LMI is not transferable between lenders. If you refinance while still above 80% LVR, the new lender generally charges a fresh premium. Partial refunds on the original policy are sometimes available within the first year or two, but many insurers do not offer them at all.

Why is this a range and not an exact number?

Because insurers do not publish their rate tables. The premium depends on your loan size, LVR, loan term, whether you are an owner-occupier or investor, and your borrower profile — and each lender uses a different insurer or in-house arrangement. Any website quoting a single exact figure is guessing. We get the real number from the lender's own table.
